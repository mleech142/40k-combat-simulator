## 40K Combat Simulator 2.2.2

### Fixes

- **Harlequins are back.** Death Jester, Solitaire, Shadowseer, Troupe, Troupe Master, Skyweavers, Starweaver and Voidweaver now appear in both **Aeldari** and **Drukhari**, as BSData lists them in both armies. The same unit showing under each faction is intended: each copy uses its own army's rules and detachments.
- **Ynnari units are back** in Aeldari: Yvraine, The Visarch, The Yncarne and the Ynnari Archon, Incubi, Kabalite Warriors, Wyches, Reavers, Succubus, Raider and Venom.
- **Yvraine and the Visarch** can now be attached to the units their Leader ability lists.
- **Saved scenarios** now reload the attacker from the faction it was saved with, rather than the first unit with that name. Legends units in saved scenarios load too.

The app re-imports your BSData files on first start to pick these up; nothing needs downloading again.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over.
