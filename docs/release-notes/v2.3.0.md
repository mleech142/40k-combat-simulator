## 40K Combat Simulator 2.3.0

### New: Play mode (experimental)

Play mode is still being built and stays hidden unless you turn it on: **Setup → Play (experimental) → Show Play in the menu**. With it off, nothing else in the app changes.

What's in it so far:

- **Full games on a top-down board** between two saved armies, following the 11th edition core rules: movement (including waypoint moves, Monsters and Vehicles moving through models, climbing Dense terrain, taking to the skies), shooting, charges, fights (eligibility, Fights First, pile in and consolidate), wound allocation with Precision, Damaged profiles, battle-shock and end-of-turn coherency.
- **Missions:** the six deployment maps, objectives, force dispositions, primary missions, Fixed and Tactical secondary missions, actions and operation markers, and Twist cards, with VP on a scoreboard and in the battle log.
- **Battlefields:** the Event Companion's 45 terrain layouts, the 11th edition terrain rules, and an editor for your own saved battlefields.
- **Battle mats** under the board: seven of them, one picked per game.
- **Online games with a friend:** play through game files, or online through a relay. Start a game with your army alone and share the invite code; the other player joins with theirs.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over.
