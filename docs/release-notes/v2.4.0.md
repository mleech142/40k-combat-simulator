## 40K Combat Simulator 2.4.0

### New: Match view for your armies

A new **Match view** button on the **Armies** page saves your list and opens everything you need at the table for it, on one page:

- **Every unit's datasheet** at the size and wargear you chose: characteristics, the weapons it carries, its abilities, keywords and enhancement, with leaders shown alongside the units they lead.
- **Your army and detachment rules**, and the **enhancements** you've taken.
- **Every stratagem your army can use** (core, faction and your chosen detachments'), with its type, whose turn and which phase it's used in. Filter them by phase and turn.
- **Printable**, for a copy beside the board.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over.
