## 40K Combat Simulator 2.2.0

### New: Armies

Build an army list, check it, and test it against anything. Open **Armies** from the menu.

- **Build:** choose a faction and battle size, then add units from the faction's datasheets, at their legal sizes.
- **Detachments:** take one or more, within the battle size's Detachment Points (Incursion 2, Strike Force 3). No two can share a Unique Tag.
- **Characters:** attach **leaders** and **support units** to the units they can join, pick **enhancements** (only the ones your detachments and that Character allow) and choose your **Warlord**.
- **Wargear:** press **Wargear** on a unit to choose its weapons in a popup ("Plague Champion: power fist", "2× Plague Marine w/ blight launcher"). Points and limits come from the datasheet, including rules like "max 1 per 5 models".
- **Checks:** the list flags anything that breaks the rules: points and Detachment Points, the rule of three (six for Battleline, one for Epic Heroes), enhancement limits, leader pairings.
- **Use it:** **Analyse as attackers** or **as defenders** opens the army in Analytics with its real wargear, leaders and enhancements. **Copy as text** gives you the list to share.

### Also new

- **Several detachments per side** on the Simulator and Analytics.
- **Enhancements count in the maths:** their effects (Lethal Hits, a 4+ invulnerable save and so on) apply in Analytics, and their points are added.
- **A user guide,** with screenshots and how the maths works in plain English, linked from the project page.

### Changed

- **Setup** puts the two downloads you need side by side as steps 1 and 2.
- **Wider pages** on big screens.

### Getting it

Download the zip below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator.exe`. Your data, armies and downloads carry over. The **first start takes about 15 seconds** while the unit data is re-read for the new features; after that it's back to normal.
