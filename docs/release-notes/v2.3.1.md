## 40K Combat Simulator 2.3.1

### Fixed

- **Play: hosting or joining an online game said "Couldn't reach the relay: check your internet connection"** on some Windows PCs. Windows had an expired security certificate stored, and the app trusted it over the valid one. The app now ships its own up-to-date certificate list for everything it downloads (BSData, Wahapedia and the relay), and if a secure connection ever fails it says so instead of blaming your internet connection.

Play mode is still experimental and hidden until you tick **Setup → Play (experimental) → Show Play in the menu**.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over.
