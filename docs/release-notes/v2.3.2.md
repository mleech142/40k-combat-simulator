## 40K Combat Simulator 2.3.2

### Fixed

- **Play: joining an online game said "Choose one of your saved armies"** without letting you choose one. Clicking **Join** now asks which of your saved armies to bring, then **Join with this army** joins the game. (You need at least one army saved on the **Armies** page.)

Play mode is still experimental and hidden until you tick **Setup → Play (experimental) → Show Play in the menu**.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over.
