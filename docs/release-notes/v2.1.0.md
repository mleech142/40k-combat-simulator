## 40K Combat Simulator 2.1.0

### New

- **Real rules and stratagems, from Wahapedia.** The Setup page has a new **Download latest** button for rules and stratagems: every faction's stratagems, detachment rules and army rules, word for word, from the [Wahapedia data export](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/the-rules/data-export) (powered by Wahapedia; not endorsed by Games Workshop). Like BSData, you download your own copy; update it any time from Setup.
- **Read into the maths.** The app reads what each stratagem does into modifiers: about two thirds of the stratagems that affect a fight, and all the core ones that do (Smokescreen, Fire Overwatch, Epic Challenge). Tick them on the Simulator and Analytics as before; they're offered with the detachment you choose.
- **Conditions are yours to judge.** When a stratagem only works in some situations ("if your unit is within range of an objective marker"), hover over it in the tick list to see when to tick it.
- **Check or fix any of them** on the Data tab: each one shows its full text and how much the app could read. Change what it applies to, its phase or its modifier, and Reset goes back to what was read. Your changes are kept when you download again.

### Changed

- **Damage distribution chart.** It now shows only in Monte Carlo mode (the normal mode works with averages, so it has no spread to show; the chart there was a placeholder). Bars run from no damage to the most any run dealt, instead of lumping everything above 18 into one bar that often went missing, and a dashed line marks the target's total wounds. Same in the Analytics cell detail and the Scenarios comparison.
- The core stratagems that shipped with the app are replaced by Wahapedia's once you download them.

### Getting it

Download the zip below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator.exe`. Your data and BSData catalogues carry over; go to **Setup** and click the new **Download latest** under *Rules and stratagems*.
