## 40K Combat Simulator 2.8.0

### New: self-hosting for your group

Run one copy of the app on a home PC, NAS or small server, and everyone in your group opens it in a browser. Guide: [docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/mleech142/40k-combat-simulator/blob/master/docs/self-hosting.md).

- **Docker image:** `ghcr.io/mleech142/40k-combat-simulator:2.8.0` (and `:latest`), with `compose.yaml` in the repository. It listens on its own computer only. Friends come in through Tailscale's HTTPS address (recommended) or another HTTPS proxy.
- **Named players:** the first visit creates the host (it needs the setup code printed in the copy's log). The host adds players on the **Players** page, and each player gets a one-time join link to choose their password.
- **Your own lists and games:** each player's armies are their own, and everyone can see them read-only. Scenarios and comparisons are private. Play a game against another player on the same copy, each in your own browser.
- **"Your move" emails** through your own mail server (SMTP settings in the guide).
- **Proposals:** a player's change to the shared data (custom factions, units, weapons, rules, stratagems) goes to the host, who approves or rejects it on the **Proposals** page.
- **Your own Play setting:** each player chooses on their **Account** page whether Play is in their menu.

### Also new

- **Setup:** Download latest, the Wahapedia download and Re-import run in the background. The page shows each step while you wait.
- **Analytics:** wargear that changes a unit's characteristics (for example a shield's invulnerable save) now counts in the matrix, the cell detail and reverse durability, as it already did in the Match view. Play counts it too.
- **Analytics:** reverse durability gives your units your own rules', leader's and enhancements' modifiers as targets, as the Simulator does.
- **Play:** the stratagem panel shows why a stratagem can't be used now (not enough CP, already used this phase, or a reaction the game offers when it can be used).
- **Simulator:** the Death Guard Afflicted choice is gone from the Simulator page. Analytics and Play keep it.
- **Fixes:** many fixes for several people using the app at once (re-imports, saves and game moves no longer get in each other's way).

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over. The BSData import runs again on first start.
