## 40K Combat Simulator 2.4.1

### Fixed

- **Analytics: attached leaders are now named** in the matrix rows (e.g. "Tempestus Scions + Militarum Tempestus Command Squad"), so you can tell which rows include the leader's weapons and buffs. (#17)
- **Units no longer carry rules that only apply with a leader or certain wargear.** For example, the Krieg Command Squad no longer has a 4+ Feel No Pain without an Ogryn Bodyguard. This also fixes about 100 other units, such as Necron Warriors and Skitarii, and changes their results in the Simulator and Analytics. (#14)
- **Later copies of a datasheet cost more where the rules say so.** For example, the third Kasrkin unit now costs 120 points. (#15)
- **Bullgryns can choose Slabshields or Brute shields**, and the Match view shows the shield's Wounds and invulnerable save. Chosen wargear now changes the stats, abilities and keywords on Match view datasheets. (#13)
- **Daemon Princes can choose their god**, and that god decides which enhancements they can take. (#10)
- **Enhancements for non-Character units work.** Warptide's Bane-forged weapons go on Battleline units (up to 3, counting as one enhancement). Other detachments' enhancements for Armigers, Lictors, Helbrutes and similar units can now be taken too. (#11)
- **Each model now chooses its own special weapon.** In a Tempestus Command Squad you can give one model a plasma gun and another a meltagun, and the per-unit limits are checked. (#16)

**Note:** in saved armies, a weapon option chosen under a squad of several models now means one model takes it, not all of them. Check lists that used special weapons.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over. The BSData import runs again on first start.
