## 40K Combat Simulator 2.5.0

### New: Death Guard's Contagion and Plagues

- **Simulator and Analytics:** a new **Afflicted** choice for the attacker and the defender, with or without a Plague. An Afflicted unit has -1 Toughness and its Plague's effect. Bonuses that only work against Afflicted units now count when the target is Afflicted, and only then: for example the Malignant Plaguecaster's Sustained Hits 1 and the Helbrute's +1 to wound.
- **Play mode:** enemy units within Contagion Range of your Death Guard models are Afflicted automatically. The range is 3", 6" or 9" depending on the battle round, plus Lord of Poxes' and Hypervirulent Strains' bonuses, up to 12".
  - **Choosing the Plague:** pick it in deployment from your army panel. The game then applies it: cover against an Afflicted unit's shooting and -1 to hit in its melee (Skullsquirm Blight), a worse Save (Rattlejoint Ague), or worse Move, Leadership and OC (Scabrous Soulrot).
  - **On the board:** select a Death Guard unit to see its Contagion Range. Afflicted units are marked on the board and in the unit list.
  - **Plague Marines:** after they shoot, a unit they hit stays Afflicted until the start of your next turn.
  - **Verminous Haze:** gives up to two Plague Marines units Infiltrators in deployment.
- **Data page:** leader buffs have a new **When** column, so a buff can apply only against Afflicted targets.

### Play mode (experimental), also new since 2.4.0

- **Reactions:** Fire Overwatch, Heroic Intervention, Rapid Ingress and Command Re-roll are offered when they can be used. Standing orders in your army panel answer them for you.
- **Reserves and arriving:** Strategic Reserves and Deep Strike, with shading that shows where a unit may arrive. Scouts moves and Infiltrators deployment.
- **Transports:** embark and disembark (rapid, tactical and combat), start the game embarked, emergency disembarks, Firing Deck and Deadly Demise.
- **Board:** a Measure tool, drag to pan, turn units and spin models, moves that follow the path you drag, a datasheet on hover and luck notices in the battle log.
- **Rules:** more stratagem and rule text is read into effects automatically.

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and run `40kCombatSimulator` (`.exe` on Windows). Your data carries over. The BSData import runs again on first start.
