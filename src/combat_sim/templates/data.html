How to write keywords and modifiers

Weapon abilities, unit modifiers, faction rules and stratagems all use the same keywords. Write them as on this tab:

  • Separate with commas: Blast, Lethal Hits
  • Space before numbers: Melta 2, Rapid Fire 1, Sustained Hits 1
  • Dice work in values: Sustained Hits D3, Rapid Fire D6+1 (averaged)
  • Anti and Feel No Pain: Anti-Infantry 2+, Feel No Pain 5+
  • Stat changes: Hit Modifier +1, Wound Modifier -1, AP Modifier -1, Damage Modifier -1, BS Modifier -1 (minus improves BS/WS)
  • Unit keywords for Anti (e.g. Infantry) go in a unit's Keywords or Modifiers.
  • Capitals don't matter. Hit and wound modifiers are capped at ±1 in total.
