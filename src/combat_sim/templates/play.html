Play Experimental

A game between two saved armies on one PC, taking turns. Deployment: players take turns setting up a unit in their zone (12" from their own edge): pick it on the right, click the board, drag to adjust (Shift-drag moves one model). Movement: pick one of your units, choose how it moves, then drag it; the rings show how far each model can go from where it started. Move or reset units as often as you like: moves lock when you go on to the Shooting phase. Shooting: pick one of your units, click an enemy unit to target it (or choose per weapon), then Fire. Charge: pick a unit, roll 2D6, pick the units to charge, then drag it in. Fight: both players pile in, then pick units to fight in turn (the banner says whose pick), then consolidate. The mouse wheel zooms.
